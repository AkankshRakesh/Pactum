ART. I
Article I

Formation, Execution & Immutability

§1.1
Formation of Agreement
Either party may initiate a contract using BindPact's structured template engine or custom clause builder. The initiating party ("Drafter") presents the instrument to the counterparty ("Reviewer") for inspection, annotation, and negotiation.
›
§1.2
Negotiation Period
During the open period, both parties may propose edits. All modifications are timestamped, attributed, and recorded in the permanent audit log. No change may be applied without acknowledgment from both parties.
›
§1.3
Execution & Sealing
Upon execution by both verified signatories, the document is cryptographically sealed. A SHA-3 hash is generated from the final instrument. The sealed hash is the document's identity — any modification produces a different hash, making tampering self-evident.
›
§1.4
Post-Execution Immutability
The sealed instrument may not be modified by any party, including BindPact Inc. It exists as-executed, permanently. A dispute reference always returns both parties to the identical, frozen document.
›
ART. II
Article II

Platform Guarantees & Legal Standing

§2.1
Dual-Party Binding
Both Contractor and Freelancer bear equal legal obligation under the executed instrument. Neither party holds seniority of claim.
§2.2
Cryptographic Verification
The sealed hash constitutes proof of the document's exact state at time of execution. Third-party verification is available at verify.bindpact.com.
§2.3
Jurisdiction Coverage
Instruments executed on BindPact are enforceable under ESIGN, eIDAS, UETA, and equivalent statutes across US, EU, UK, CA, and AU jurisdictions.
§2.4
Source of Truth
In the event of dispute, the sealed BindPact instrument supersedes all other versions, screenshots, email threads, or verbal representations of the agreement.
§2.5
Audit Trail Integrity
All pre-execution history — drafts, edits, annotations, timestamps — is preserved in the immutable audit log alongside the sealed document.
§2.6
Platform Non-Interference
BindPact Inc. is prohibited by its own architecture from modifying, deleting, or accessing the content of any sealed instrument.
